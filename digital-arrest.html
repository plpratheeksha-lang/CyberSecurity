DIGITAL ARREST AWARENESS

Recognize the Warning Signs

Learn how scammers may use fear, urgency and impersonation to pressure people into giving money or sensitive information.
🚨

What is a Digital Arrest Scam?

A scammer may falsely claim to be a police officer, government official or another authority. They may threaten the victim with arrest or legal action and demand money or sensitive information.

WARNING SIGNS

Watch for These Red Flags

📞

Fake Authority

Someone claims to be from the police, court, government or another authority without reliable verification.

⚠️

Immediate Threat

You are told that you will be arrested or face legal action unless you act immediately.

💰

Money Demand

The caller asks you to transfer money, pay a fine or deposit money to avoid an alleged legal problem.

🔐

Sensitive Information

You are pressured to reveal OTPs, passwords, banking information or identity details.

AWARENESS SIMULATION

What Would You Do?

Read the situation and choose the safest response.
📞 Incoming Video Call

"We are calling from Cyber Crime Police. Your identity has been linked to a serious financial investigation. Your arrest order is being prepared. Stay on this video call and transfer money immediately to prove that you are innocent."

What should you do?

SAFETY CHECKLIST

If You Receive a Suspicious Call

01

Stay Calm

Do not make rushed decisions because someone is threatening or pressuring you.

02

End the Call

Do not remain on a suspicious video or phone call under pressure.

03

Verify Independently

Use an official contact method rather than the number or link supplied by the caller.

04

Don't Transfer Money

Do not send money or reveal OTPs, passwords or banking information.
