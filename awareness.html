CYBERSECURITY EDUCATION

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Learn simple practices to identify suspicious online activity and protect your personal information.

Why Cyber Awareness Matters

Cyber scams often use urgency, fear, attractive offers or fake authority to make people act quickly. Learning to recognize these warning signs can help users make safer decisions before clicking links, sharing information or installing applications.

🎣

Phishing Awareness

Phishing attempts try to trick users into revealing sensitive information or visiting unsafe websites.

  • Check the sender before responding.
  • Be careful with unexpected links.
  • Look for suspicious or deceptive URLs.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords.
🚨

Digital Arrest Scam

Scammers may pretend to be police officers, government officials or other authorities and use fear and urgency.

  • Do not panic because of an unexpected call.
  • Do not transfer money because of threats.
  • Independently verify the identity.
  • Do not share confidential information.
📱

Malicious Application Safety

Some applications may request permissions that are unnecessary for their stated purpose.

  • Download applications from trusted sources.
  • Review requested permissions.
  • Avoid unknown APK files.
  • Remove applications you do not trust.
🔐

Password & Account Safety

Strong account security reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Avoid reusing important passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Never share passwords with others.
💳

Online Payment Safety

Be cautious when receiving unexpected payment requests or refund offers.

  • Verify the recipient before making a payment.
  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.
  • Be careful with unexpected QR codes.
  • Verify refund and payment requests independently.
🌐

Safe Browsing

Safe browsing habits can help users avoid deceptive websites and suspicious downloads.

  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Avoid suspicious pop-ups.
  • Keep your browser updated.
  • Do not download unknown files.

⚠️ Common Warning Signs

🚩 Unexpected urgent message
🚩 Threats of account blocking or arrest
🚩 Request for OTP or password
🚩 Request for immediate payment
🚩 Suspicious website address
🚩 Unknown application requesting excessive permissions
🚩 Unusually attractive offer or prize
🚩 Pressure to keep the conversation secret

🛡️ What Should You Do?

01 STOP

Do not click, pay or share information immediately.

02 VERIFY

Check the information using an official source.

03 REPORT

Report suspicious activity through appropriate official channels.
